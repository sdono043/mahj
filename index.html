Bar Setup Notes

  • Aperol is shared between the Spritz and the Paper Plane — one bottle covers both.
  • Garnish tray: lime wheels, orange slices & peel, lemon twists, mint, rosemary sprigs, grapefruit wheel.
  • Keep tequila, gin, rum, and bourbon each in their own labeled bottle at the station for self-serve.
  • Bar sign: same kraft-paper-and-paint-pen treatment as the grazing table — list all 7 drinks on a small propped-up sign so it reads as one self-serve station.
  • Ice: ~70–75 lbs total for 48 guests over a 4–5 hour party (drinks plus chilling wine/cava separately) — roughly 3–4 store bags.
  • Cups: budget 2–3 drinks per guest, so 100–150 cups. Stemless plastic wine glasses for the Spritzes and wine, rocks cups for everything else.

Sangria — How to Make It

Red (per bottle, batch ×3): 1 bottle red wine, ½ cup brandy, ¼ cup triple sec, ¼ cup sugar, sliced orange/apple/berries. Chill at least 4 hours, overnight is better. Top with club soda right before serving.

White (per bottle, batch ×3): 1 bottle white wine, ½ cup brandy, ¼ cup triple sec, ¼ cup sugar, sliced lemon/peach/grapes. Chill overnight, top with club soda or lemon-lime soda right before serving.

Notes

  • Based on ~40–42 drinking guests averaging ~4 drinks each over the night (~180 total pours across cocktails, sangria & beer) — scale this up or down as RSVPs firm up.
  • Amaro Nonino is the priciest single item for a small amount used — swap in Ramazzotti or Averna if you want to save there.
  • Pre-batch the Negroni, White Negroni, and both sangrias the day before — one less thing to do on the day.
  • 8 bottles of champagne/cava gives ~50–64 pours — enough for a real toast pour (not just a sip) for every guest at 8:30 PM, plus extra for anyone who wants it as their drink for the night. Chill it alongside the wine so it's ready by the reveal.
  • This shopping list covers alcohol, mixers & garnish only — ice and cups are already covered in Bar Setup Notes above.
