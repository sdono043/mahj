Bar Setup Notes

  • Pre-batch the Negroni ahead of time — it's equal-parts and stirred, so just pour over ice.
  • Aperol is shared between the Spritz and the Paper Plane — one bottle covers both.
  • Garnish tray: lime wheels, orange slices & peel, lemon twists, rosemary sprigs, grapefruit wheel.
  • Keep tequila, gin, and bourbon each in their own labeled bottle at the station for self-serve.
  • Bar sign: same kraft-paper-and-paint-pen treatment as the grazing table — list all 5 drinks on a small propped-up sign so it reads as one self-serve station.
  • Ice: ~70–75 lbs total for 48 guests over a 4–5 hour party (drinks plus chilling wine/cava separately) — roughly 3–4 store bags.
  • Cups: budget 2–3 drinks per guest, so 100–150 cups. Stemless plastic wine glasses for the Spritzes and wine, rocks cups for everything else.
